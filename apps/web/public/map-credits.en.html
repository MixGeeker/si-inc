Map data and credits

Surface and boundaries

The game groups the mainland, Taiwan and the Spratly Islands into one selectable map feature called China. The islands use 21 separate outlines from the Natural Earth 1:10m dataset. Selecting this feature opens the Asian market. Natural Earth 1:10m Admin 0

Land colors come from the November 2004 Blue Marble: Next Generation imagery by Reto Stöckli and colleagues at NASA Earth Observatory. The game blends the imagery with Natural Earth I, adjusts the colors and resamples it. Country outlines come from Natural Earth 1:50m Admin 0 Countries, converted to SVG. Natural Earth data is in the public domain. NASA Blue Marble · Natural Earth terms of use

Nighttime lights come from NASA Earth Observatory’s 2016 Black Marble global grayscale composite, based on nighttime VIIRS observations from the Suomi NPP satellite. The game renders the lights in warm tones. NASA Earth at Night maps

Terrain and ocean depths come from Mapzen Terrain Tiles, accessed on November 13, 2026. Level 3 Terrarium tiles were reprojected onto a latitude–longitude elevation grid. Ocean colors are based on depth. Source data · Full elevation data credits

City lights come from the 2016 NASA Earth Observatory Black Marble grayscale composite of Suomi NPP VIIRS observations. The game tints the observed lights a warm color. NASA night light maps

Wind, humidity and currents

NCEP/NCAR Reanalysis 1 data is provided by the NOAA Physical Sciences Laboratory in Boulder, Colorado. Wind and relative humidity come from the 1991–2020 November climatology at 850 hPa. Cloud shapes are procedurally generated. NOAA PSL

This study used data collected and made freely available by the NOAA Global Drifter Program. Ocean currents use the November mean drifter velocity field from NOAA/AOML and the University of Miami, version 3.10, with observations through February 28, 2023. The game samples every fourth grid point and quantizes velocities to millimeters per second. Eastward and northward velocities drive the surface traces. Dataset authors are Laurindo, Mariano and Lumpkin (2017). NOAA data and metadata · Research paper · CC BY 4.0
